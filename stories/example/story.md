## mouth

Cold air pushes out of the opening in the rock, steady as breath. Behind you
the trail is already losing itself in the dusk. You have a lantern with maybe
an hour of oil in it, and a decision to make while there is still light enough
to make it badly.

[[Go in->tunnel]]
[[Follow the trail back down->trail]]

## tunnel

The passage narrows twice and then, without warning, opens. Your lantern
finds a floor of pale sand and, twenty feet ahead, a place where the sand has
been disturbed — a long, dragged furrow leading deeper in.

[[Follow the furrow->furrow]]
[[Leave, quickly->mouth]]

## furrow

The furrow ends at a pack. Canvas, old, half-buried. Someone set it down
carefully — the straps are coiled, not flung — and then did not come back for
it. Inside: a tin of matches, dry, and a folded map with one corner torn away.

[[Take the map and go deeper->deeper]]
[[Take the matches and leave->trail]]

## deeper [ending]

The map is wrong about the first turn and right about everything after it.
Four hours later you come out of a crack in the hillside two valleys over,
lantern long dead, matches gone, holding a piece of paper that somebody else
died making.

You keep the map. You do not go back.

## trail [ending]

The trail finds itself again below the treeline, and the cave keeps whatever
it was keeping.

Some nights that is enough. Most nights it is not, and you lie there and think
about the cold air pushing out of the rock, steady as breath.
